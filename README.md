# Species-specific bias in national stem-bark allometry for the larches (*Larix*)

Manuscript, data and analysis for a component-level assessment of Canada's national
stem-bark biomass coefficients in the two North American larches, and a test of what the
bias costs British Columbia's larch bark carbon account.


---

## Structure of the manuscript

Section order follows the *Forest Science* requirement for original research:

| Section | Contains |
|---|---|
| Introduction | generalized equations and their known cost; why bark, why western larch |
| Study Area and Methods | Biomass data · Development of allometric equations · Bark geometry from stem taper · Application to the forest inventory · Published comparison equation |
| Results | National equation accuracy · Larch-specific equations · Bark allocation · Carbon stocks · Comparison with published equations |
| Discussion | mechanism, spatial coherence, bounds on the extrapolation, limitations |
| Management Applications | what a corrected coefficient would change for MRV and offsets |
| Data availability, References | |

Subsection headings follow **Delcourt et al. 2022**, the closest exemplar: they name the
object studied, not the analytical stage.

**11 tables and 9 figures**, each placed at its point of citation. All are generated by the
analysis, never typed.
