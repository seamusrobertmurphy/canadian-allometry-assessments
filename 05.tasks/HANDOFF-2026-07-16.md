## The turning point
ENFOR (the open dataset the national coefficients were fitted on) contains **916 tamarack
larch (*Larix laricina*) in western Canada (AB + MB + NT + SK)**, each with complete
oven-dry stem-bark mass (`OM_stem_bark`, kg), DBH, and height. Open (OGL-Canada;
DOI 10.23687/fbad665e-8ac9-4635-9f84-e4fd53a6253c). That is a large, clean, open bark
sample — but it is tamarack, not western larch, and it is the coefficient's own calibration
data, so the analysis must be framed as an adequacy / residual + refit-and-cross-validate
study (and can contrast western-Canada vs national fit), not a naive "predicted vs
independent truth."

## Decision to make first (scope)
- **A. Pivot species to tamarack larch (*L. laricina*), western Canada, on ENFOR (n≈916).** Best data, fully open. Recommended.
- **B. Keep western larch (*L. occidentalis*).** Needs Affleck INGY (470 trees to 105 cm, by request; not open); open data thin (LegacyTreeData: 13 stem-bark trees ≤20 cm).
- **C. Genus-level larch bark bias:** ENFOR tamarack + western larch (Affleck/LegacyTreeData).
